PawsitivID

Your pet's face is their ID.

What it is

PawsitivID is a pet identification, licensing, and lost-and-found platform. A few photos become a biometric ID - face, nose print, coat pattern - that can identify a pet from a single picture taken on any phone.

Owners register pets and handle city licensing digitally; anyone who finds a lost pet can report it without even creating an account, and the system matches it back to its owner.

Why it exists

Microchips only help when a shelter or vet scans them - and the person who finds a lost dog usually has nothing but a phone. PawsitivID turns that phone into the scanner.

It is built at Tuul, where I head product - my part runs from the product specs to how the licensing, matching, and authority dashboards come together.

What it gets you

For owners: register your pet once and they are findable anywhere - a found-pet report triggers an instant match and notification.

For cities and animal-control teams: licensing and lost-and-found cases in one dashboard instead of paper trails.
